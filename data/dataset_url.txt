WildlifeReID-10k dataset:
https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/wildlifedatasets/wildlifereid-10k

The full dataset is intentionally excluded from Git. The application can use:

1. A local extracted copy at data/wildlifereid-10k, or the path in
   WILDLIFE_DATASET_ROOT.
2. A local archive at data/archive.zip, or the path in
   WILDLIFE_DATASET_ARCHIVE.
3. Kaggle on-demand mode (enabled by default), which downloads metadata and
   only the test images requested by the application.
